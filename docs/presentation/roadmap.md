## Roadmap Directions
- **Deeper QC**: Image-based pre/post-contrast and intensity quality control.
- **More Modalities**: First-class CT and PET, including photon-counting CT.
- **NILS Agent**: Local natural-language querying and operation of NILS.
- **Agentic Curation**: Intelligent, site-aware keyword tuning with humans in the loop.
- **Pipeline Ecosystem**: Curated templates plus community-imported analyses.
- **Unified Query & Robust Local AI**: Manual + agentic query over one data layer, powered by a hardened on-premise model.
